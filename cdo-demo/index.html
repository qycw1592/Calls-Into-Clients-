Before CIC

Good leads can live in different places.

  • Calls, texts, forms, social inquiries, and email can require separate attention.
  • Project photos and appointment preferences may arrive in different threads.
  • Estimate follow-up depends on someone remembering the right customer at the right time.
  • The owner may need to ask around to see what is stuck.
With CIC

One record follows the entire opportunity.

  • Immediate response and CDO-specific qualification.
  • Photos, project details, and measurement preferences stay organized.
  • Estimate recovery happens systematically, with human escalation when needed.
  • The owner sees leads, open estimates, follow-up, and recovered revenue in one view.
